## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Implementation or configuration is complete.

## Evidence

Source SHA, pipeline/job URLs, dataset/snapshot version and observed results. Separate local,
staging, production and live-provider results. Do not include secrets, raw traces or user prompts.

## Release boundaries

Preserve reviewed labels, thresholds, all gates, existing data and the VM. Human merge and
production approval are required. A product regression repair needs its own explicit scope.
